# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Filipino homeowners, condo residents, and renters who need trusted everyday or emergency home services (locksmith, plumbing, electrical, aircon cleaning/repair, and general home repairs) without haggling or worrying about who enters their home.

## Product Purpose

Serbify is a Filipino on-demand home services app that lets customers book verified local service pros in minutes with clear upfront pricing, live arrival tracking, and safe in-app payments.

## Positioning

- Upfront pricing with no hidden fees (inspections starting from ₱80)
- Government ID and NBI-verified local technicians
- Payment held safely until the customer confirms the job is done (supports GCash, Maya, card, or cash)
- Live arrival tracking and private in-app chat/call without sharing personal phone numbers

## Operating Context

- Customers pin their exact barangay/address, pick a service or compare upfront prices, track their pro's arrival in real time, review photo proof of the finished work, and pay securely.
- Plain, everyday English/Filipino-context language—no technical jargon (no PSGC, WebSocket, WebRTC, 3D liveness KYC, PayMongo, deterministic state, or escrow-rail terminology).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Customer-only focus on the showcase website (no "For Pros" mode or dual-sided switcher).
- Pre-launch state: do not display unverified job counts, star rating totals, or monetary property-protection guarantees (no `24,800+ jobs`, `25,000+ homeowners`, `4.9★`, or `₱10,000 protection`).

## Brand Commitments

- Mascot and brand logo in `public/assets/`.

- No confirmed launch metrics, testimonials, or app store ratings yet (omit fabricated numbers).

## Product Principles

1. **Plain-spoken clarity:** Every headline, step, and label must be immediately understood by any homeowner—never expose internal engineering or payment-rail terms.
2. **Trust before arrival:** Show ID and NBI verification, upfront pricing, and private in-app communication before a stranger steps into the home.
3. **Calm control from booking to sign-off:** Keep the customer informed at each step—price upfront, live ETA on the way, photo check when done, and payment released only on approval.
4. **Honest pre-launch proof:** Demonstrate the real booking experience and product UI rather than invented stats or guarantees.
